Our story

Every thread ties a celebration together

In Hindi, dor means thread, the thing that ties everything together. Dor Creation is a women-owned studio in Agrawal Nagar, Indore, dressing the people who make a wedding complete: the bride's side.

From coordinated bridesmaid looks to modern indo-western outfits, we help every guest feel part of the moment, and look it in every photo.
