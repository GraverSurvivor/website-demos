The family

A whole family, one craft.

At B.M. Sitarmaker the whole family works on the instruments: shaping the wood, fitting the gourd, setting the frets, tuning and decorating. The father still does the tanpura decoration work himself.

Miraj has been making sitars and tanpuras for generations, and customers come to this workshop for three things: the sound, the finish, and the care taken over every instrument before it leaves.
