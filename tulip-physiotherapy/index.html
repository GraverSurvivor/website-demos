Our speciality

Knee replacement recovery, step by step.

Knee replacement recovery is what our patients mention most in their reviews. Recovery is measured in how far your knee can bend. Drag the slider to see what each stage lets you do again.

0°Straight leg, right after surgery
First weeks

Settle the knee

Ease swelling and pain, get the knee straightening and bending, and start walking with support.

Next

Build strength

Strengthen the thigh and hip muscles, walk further, and depend less on the walker or stick.

Then

Stairs & balance

Practise stairs, balance and longer walks until you feel confident moving on your own.

After that

Back to life

Return to daily routines with a home exercise plan that keeps your new knee strong.

Angles and phases are general guidance only. Every recovery is different, and your physiotherapist sets your plan after assessing you.

Questions

Good to know.

Do you offer physiotherapy at home?

Yes. Home visits are ideal after knee or hip replacement surgery, when travelling is difficult. Tell us your area in Nashik when you book.

The clinic is on the 3rd floor. Can I get up there after surgery?

Yes, there's a lift in Genesis Apartment, so you won't need to use the stairs.

When should I start physiotherapy after knee replacement?

Your surgeon will advise you, and rehab usually begins soon after the operation. Get in touch before you're discharged so we can plan your first sessions together.

What should I bring to my first session?

Your surgery or discharge papers and any recent reports, a list of your medicines, and loose, comfortable clothing that lets us see and move the knee or affected area.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your condition, your surgery and your goals. We'll assess you in the first session and explain the plan and how we'll track your progress.
