The craft

A craft Kolhapur is famous for.

The Kolhapuri chappal has a Geographical Indication (GI) tag, which recognises it as a traditional craft of Kolhapur and the surrounding districts. Designers around the world have discovered it lately. We still make ours the traditional way.

There are many traditional styles, each with its own strap and finish. Ask us which ones we have ready today.

Care

Make them last.

Good leather lasts for years if you treat it right. Three simple habits:

☀️

Dry in the shade

If they get wet, let them dry naturally in the shade, not in direct sun or near a heater, so the leather doesn't crack.

🫙

Oil them now and then

A little oil rubbed into the leather every few weeks keeps it soft and supple. Ask us what we use.

👣

Let them break in

The first few days they'll feel firm. Keep wearing them and they'll mould to the shape of your feet.
