Our story

Udupu means clothing. We make it yours.

In Kannada, udupu simply means clothing. At Udupu Designer Studio in Jayanagar, we believe clothing only becomes yours when it fits you perfectly.

This women-owned studio is the go-to place for many customers who want good quality stitching, and the compliments that come with it. Bring your fabric and your idea, and we'll do the rest.
